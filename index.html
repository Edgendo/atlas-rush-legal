Privacy Policy

Atlas Rush: Flag Quiz · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Atlas Rush: Flag Quiz (“the app”) is a flag and geography quiz game made by IronLogic (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses and who handles it.

In short: we have no accounts and no servers that store your data. Your progress stays on your phone. The only companies that handle data are Google (for optional Google Play Games sign-in, for the optional rewarded video ads, and for the Play Store itself).

What stays on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only. We do not receive it, and we have no servers that store it:

  • Your progress: level, XP, hint points, play tokens, best scores, streaks, the countries you have answered, and how far you got in the Daily Challenge.
  • Your settings: light or dark mode, sound effects and vibration.
  • Your birth year, which the app asks for once when you first open it. It is used only on your phone to decide which features and which kind of ads suit your age. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it cannot be changed afterwards.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your phone’s settings, removes all of this.

Ads

The app can show optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, but only when you choose to watch one (for example to earn an extra play token or a hint point). There are no banner ads and no ads that appear on their own.

To show an ad, AdMob may collect information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address and details about the device and about how the ad is shown. Google is responsible for this data. See the Google Privacy Policy and how AdMob uses data.

  • Players who might be under 13 (based on the birth year you give) only see child-directed, non-personalised ads with content suitable for all ages.
  • In the EU, UK and similar regions you are asked for your consent choices before any ad is requested, and you can change them at any time in Settings under “Ad privacy choices”.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Google Play Games (optional, players aged 13 and over)

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, Google handles your sign-in and shows your scores on leaderboards. Your player name and scores are then visible to other players. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. The app does not offer sign-in, scores or leaderboards to players who say they are under 13. You can play everything without signing in.

Purchases

The app does not currently sell anything. If in-app purchases are added later, they will be handled by Google Play, we will not receive your payment details, and we will update this policy.

Children

Atlas Rush is made for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not ask for names, email addresses or locations. Players who might be under 13 get no Google Play Games features, only child-directed ads, and no personalised advertising.

What we do not do

  • We have no user accounts.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We do not sell or share your data.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Your choices and rights

  • Switch sound and vibration off in Settings.
  • Play without signing in to Google Play Games.
  • Decline ads. They are optional, and the game works without watching any.
  • Change your ad consent in Settings (“Ad privacy choices”) where it is offered.
  • Delete everything the app stores by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, you can use the controls in your Google Account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also tell you in the app.
